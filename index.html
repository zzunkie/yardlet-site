Yardlet

A native macOS workbench for coding agents.

Yardlet is a personal desktop app for macOS. It runs coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity on your own Mac and shows their sessions in one window. Each agent keeps its own sign-in and history.

Google services

If you choose to connect a Google account, Yardlet lets the coding agents you run read and work with your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. You sign in with Google in your browser; Yardlet never sees your password. The sign-in is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac, and Yardlet calls Google's APIs directly from your Mac. There is no Yardlet server.
